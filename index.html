How it works

Three steps, no cost and no obligation. You see the finished design before anything gets built.

01

Request your free design

Tell us what you're thinking for your backyard and when it's easiest for us to visit. It takes about a minute.

02

We walk your yard together

A free in-home consultation, about 45 minutes. We measure, listen to how you want to use the space and talk through what to keep.

03

See it in 3D

We design your yard in zones and show you the 3D concept, so you can make decisions with confidence.

Design in zones, not just a yard

The most used backyards give people somewhere to go. We plan each area around how your family actually lives, built for Valley heat and Valley nights.

ZONE 1

Dining

Right off the kitchen door, under shade, where meals and parties happen.

ZONE 2

Lounge

Facing the view, not the fence, with shade from the west sun.

ZONE 3

Fire

At the back of the yard, so there's always somewhere to walk to once it cools off.

ZONE 4

Lawn

A smart strip of real grass for kids and pets, with low-water planting around it.

Questions

Is the 3D design really free?

Yes. The design and the in-home consultation are free, with no obligation to buy anything.

What happens at the consultation?

We walk your yard with you for about 45 minutes, take measurements and talk about how you want to use the space. Then we create your 3D design concept.

Which areas do you serve?

The San Fernando Valley (Encino, Sherman Oaks, Studio City, Tarzana, Woodland Hills and nearby), the Conejo Valley (Calabasas, Agoura Hills, Westlake Village, Thousand Oaks) and anywhere in greater Los Angeles.

How do your text messages work?

When you request a free design through our website chat or our Facebook/Instagram form and agree to texts, we text you about your request: scheduling, reminders, follow-ups and occasional offers. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP at any time to stop all texts, or HELP for help. Your phone number is never sold or shared with third parties for marketing.

Are the designs shown real projects?

Images in our ads and on our pages are design concepts shown for illustration.
