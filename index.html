Questions

Is the 3D design really free?

Yes. The design and the in-home consultation are free, with no obligation to buy anything.

What happens at the consultation?

We walk your yard with you for about 45 minutes, take measurements and talk about how you want to use the space. Then we create your 3D design concept.

Which areas do you serve?

The San Fernando Valley (Encino, Sherman Oaks, Studio City, Tarzana, Woodland Hills and nearby), the Conejo Valley (Calabasas, Agoura Hills, Westlake Village, Thousand Oaks) and anywhere in greater Los Angeles.

How do your text messages work?

If you opt in, we text you about your design request: scheduling, reminders and follow-ups. If you also opt in to marketing texts, you may get occasional design ideas and offers. Reply STOP at any time to stop all texts, or HELP for help. Your phone number is never sold or shared with third parties for marketing.
